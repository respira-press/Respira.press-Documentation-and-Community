## What Focus is

Focus is an opt-in mode. The AI app sees six tools instead of the full list, and finds any other one by searching for it. Every tool still passes the same safety checks.

The full guide is at [respira.press/docs/focus](https://www.respira.press/docs/focus). It covers how to switch Focus on for the npm server, Claude Desktop, claude.ai and ChatGPT.

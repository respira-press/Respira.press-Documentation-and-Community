# Team Accounts

A team account lets more than one person work under a single Respira account. The team owns the licences, and therefore the sites and packs. Members connect their own agents to those sites without needing their own separate subscription.

Every existing account is already a team of one. Team features only surface once a second person is invited, and a single-user account behaves exactly as it did before.

## Which plans include Teams

| Plan | Included | Further seats |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maker, and legacy Starter | The owner only. These are single-user plans without Teams. | Not available |
| Builder (including Builder 5), and legacy Agency | The owner | Unlimited invitations, €10 for each seat in use per month |
| Studio | The owner and one additional seat, with any permission | Unlimited invitations, €10 for each further seat in use per month |
| Founder and Founder Studio Plus | The owner and unlimited seats | Included |

The owner is always included and never counts as a seat. Legacy Starter keeps its one-site allowance.

## Permissions

Each person gets one of three permissions, chosen on the Team page of the dashboard:

- **View only**: the person's agent is capped to read. It can inspect sites but not write.
- **Edit**: read and write on the sites the person is granted.
- **Administrator**: edit access to sites, plus managing team access.

All three cost the same. The permission changes what someone can do, not the price. Use the team's site access settings to choose which sites each person sees.

An administrator can manage team access. Only the owner sets up seat payments and changes the billing email.

The permission is enforced at token introspection. When a member's agent connects, Respira checks that member's live permission for the team that owns the site and hands the plugin the matching scope. The plugin only honours the scope introspection returns, so moving someone to view only takes effect on their next connection without any change to the plugin or the site keys. Changes land within the plugin's roughly one-minute introspection cache.

## Per-member tokens

A member's token keeps that member's own user id for revocation and audit, while pointing at the team's existing site and licence. Two consequences follow from that:

- Removing a member revokes only that member's tokens. The team's site-connection keys never rotate, so other members and the connected sites are unaffected.
- Each member's MCP setup codes carry the team's sites through that member's own token. A member gets working setup codes for the team sites without the owner having to reshare a site key.

## Inviting people

Invitations are sent by email from the Team page of the dashboard. Each member's edits appear in the team audit log under their own identity, so you can see who changed what across every connected site.

## Joining by company email

Instead of inviting each person, an owner or administrator can let everyone at the company join on their own. On the Team page, under **Join by company email**, add the domain your team's addresses use, for example `yourcompany.com`. From then on, anyone who signs in to respira.press with a verified address on that domain joins your team with the **Edit** permission, and can open Respira on the team's sites within a few minutes. Nobody has to send an invitation.

A few rules keep this safe:

- **Only your own domain.** You can add a domain that you, the owner or an administrator of the team has an email address on. Each domain can belong to one team only.
- **No public email providers.** Domains such as gmail.com, outlook.com or icloud.com are refused, because anyone can have an address there.
- **Verified addresses only.** A person joins after signing in with a magic link or with Google, which proves they own the address.
- **Removed means removed.** Someone you removed from the team is never added back automatically. To bring them back, invite them again.
- **Edit, not more.** People who join get the Edit permission. Change it on the Team page like any other seat.

Each person who joins is a normal team seat, with the same 30 free days and the same €10 for a month in use described below. On Founder and Founder Studio Plus, seats are included, so joining costs nothing. Joining by company email is not available on Maker and legacy Starter.

Removing a domain stops new people from joining. Everyone who already joined stays on the team until you remove them. Every join, and every domain added or removed, appears in the team trail.

## 30 free days for each person

Each invited person gets 30 calendar days free, counted from the day they first activate their account.

- A pending invitation starts no clock and creates no charge.
- Resending an invitation, changing a permission, removing someone and inviting them again never restarts the 30 days.

## When a seat costs €10

After a person's 30 free days end, a seat counts as in use for a month when that person views, edits or administers anything in the workspace during that calendar month (UTC).

- A seat in use costs a flat €10 for the month, including a view-only seat. There is no proration by days.
- A seat nobody uses that month is dormant and costs €0.
- A person who used the workspace earlier in a month still counts for that month after they are removed.

On Studio, the included seat covers one seat that would otherwise be billed. Dormant seats, exempt seats and seats still inside their 30 free days do not use it up.

Existing comped arrangements are honoured.

## Setting up seat payments

The owner sets up seat payments in Billing, as a separate step. Seats are billed through their own Polar seat subscription. Your website licence and its price stay the same, and so does its renewal.

A legacy Agency licence bought through Lemon Squeezy stays in Lemon Squeezy. The owner sets up the separate seat subscription in Polar for team seats only.

Until payments are set up, a seat that would be billed pauses when its 30 free days end. Included seats, on Studio and Founder, keep working.

## Billing email

You can add an optional billing email in Settings or Billing, for example your finance contact. It receives copies of invoices and payment notices. It does not need a team seat, does not change your sign-in email, and does not receive marketing or sign-in mail.

## Client feedback

There is no separate client seat or client price any more. For comments on a page, share an expiring [Murmur](https://www.respira.press/murmur) link: your client opens the page and leaves notes without a Respira account or a team seat. If a client needs their own Respira account, invite them as a normal seat with the permission they need.

## Existing teams

Every existing team gets 30 days' notice before these rules apply, and nothing is charged retroactively.

On Maker and legacy Starter, existing collaborators keep their access during the 30-day notice. After that, the owner can move to Builder or above to keep a team, or share [Murmur](/murmur) links for page feedback. Murmur is part of Builder and up, and of legacy Starter.

# Usage limits

Respira's limits exist to stop a runaway agent, not to charge for normal work. These are the numbers.

## Requests per API key

| Limit | Default | Where to change it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Requests per key, per hour | 1,000 | **Respira → Settings → Rate limit**, or `wp option update respira_rate_limit <number>` |

An active session rarely passes 200 calls an hour. A key that reaches the limit gets a clear "rate limited" error and works again as the hour rolls on.

## Browser agents (WebMCP)

| Limit | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Runs of one ability, per person, per minute | 30 |
| Runs of all abilities, per person, per minute | 60 |

Site owners can change both with the `wmcp_rate_limit` and `wmcp_rate_limit_global_ceiling` filters.

## Respira Exhale (HTML to WordPress)

Two page conversions a month for every site your plan allows, counted per account across the dashboard, Respira AER and MCP:

| Plan | Pages a month |
| --- | --- |
| Maker (1 site) | 2 |
| Builder 5 | 10 |
| Builder 10 | 20 |
| Studio 25 | 50 |
| Studio 50 | 100 |
| Studio 100 | 200 |
| Studio 250 | 500 |
| Trial | 10 for the whole trial |

Annual plans get fifteen months of pages up front, to spend whenever. When the allowance runs out, the refusal names the plan that includes more.

## Respira AER cloud and fleet runs

Jobs that run unattended in the cloud, or across more than one site at once, count against a monthly allowance. Attended single-site work and approval decisions do not count.

| Plan | Cloud and fleet jobs a month |
| --- | --- |
| Maker | Not included |
| Builder 5 | 20 |
| Builder 10 | 40 |
| Studio (any size) | 150 |

Annual plans get one and a half times these numbers. A trial gets the Builder allowance. The allowance resets on the first of each month, UTC.

## Respira Lite

The free plugin includes 30 edits a month, plus 10 grace edits, and warns you at 80% of the allowance.

## What is not limited

On a paid plan, edits, reads, snapshots and restores through your assistant have no monthly cap. The only ceiling is the hourly request limit per key above.

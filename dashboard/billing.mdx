## Billing

The [Billing page](https://www.respira.press/dashboard/billing) at `dashboard/billing` shows your plan, the next renewal date, your invoices and the add-ons on your account. This page explains what each choice does to your access and your money. The prices are on the [pricing page](https://www.respira.press/pricing).

Plans (Maker, Builder and Studio) bill monthly or yearly and renew automatically. Yearly billing costs 34% less than twelve months of monthly billing. Nothing is charged per edit: see [Usage limits](/security/usage-limits) for the few limits that exist. Founder Lifetime Access is a one-time purchase and never renews.

Paid subscriptions are billed through Polar. Annual licences from the launch period were billed through Lemon Squeezy, and those are managed in the Lemon Squeezy portal that the Billing page links to.

## Invoices

Every invoice for a Polar plan is listed on the Billing page under **Invoices**, and each row downloads as a PDF. You can add a separate billing email in Settings or Billing, for example your finance contact. It receives copies of invoices and payment notices without taking a team seat.

## Pausing for a month

Not every month has WordPress work in it. If your plan bills monthly through Polar, press **Pause one month** on the **Pause or cancel** card.

- The month you already paid for runs to its end as usual.
- The next month has no bill and no access. Nothing is deleted: your sites, settings and site memory wait for you.
- Everything resumes by itself at the next billing cycle.
- You can pause once in any 365 days.

A yearly plan has no monthly invoice to skip, so a pause shifts the renewal date instead. Email [word@respira.press](mailto:word@respira.press) and I move the renewal by a month by hand.

## Cancelling

Press **Cancel my plan** on the **Pause or cancel** card. It asks for one optional reason, then opens the Polar portal where you confirm.

- A cancellation stops the renewal. Access continues through the end of the period you already paid for, then stops by itself. There are no further charges.
- Until that date the Billing page shows **Ends soon** and the day service ends. You can undo the cancellation from the same card, or pause instead, which also undoes it.
- Your live WordPress site is untouched. Existing pages keep serving, and your snapshots and audit log stay in your own WordPress database. See [If your plan ends or a payment fails](/safety-philosophy#if-your-plan-ends-or-a-payment-fails).

## A declined payment

A declined renewal never ends access by itself. The bank may be saying "try again later", so the plan keeps working while the payment is retried, and I email you. When the payment goes through, nothing has changed.

## Add-ons

The WooCommerce add-on costs EUR 95 a year on Maker and Builder, and Studio and Founder include it. [Respira Code](/respira-code) costs EUR 20 a month or EUR 158 a year. It is included at no extra cost on yearly Builder 10, yearly Studio and the legacy Agency plan while the 100 launch licences last, and Founder licences have it. It is not available on a trial. A cancelled yearly plan that is still inside its paid period at launch keeps Respira Code until that period ends.

## Refunds

Maker, Builder and Studio subscriptions, and add-ons, have a 14-day refund window, no questions asked. Email [word@respira.press](mailto:word@respira.press) within 14 days of the payment. Maker also starts with a 7-day free trial before the first charge, so the trial and the refund window come one after the other.

No refund is due where Respira Code access or a licence is ended for breach of the acceptable use section of the Terms (section 5.4). Founder Lifetime Access is a final sale and is not refundable, as it says at the point of purchase. The full wording is in section 12 of the [Terms of Service](https://www.respira.press/terms#payments).

## Price changes

A price change reaches existing subscribers at the next renewal, and only after at least 30 days of notice. The price you pay is locked for the period you have already paid for.
